# Polling OCR mapping checkpoint — 23 September 2026

- The OCR worker preserves the official PDF hash and URL, page text, word boxes, confidence and quality. The strict row mapper stores only **unverified proposals** when all candidate cells and printed valid/rejected/NOTA/total values are present and reconcile. It never supplies missing zeroes or totals.
- A read-only check of 100 Tamil Nadu Form 20 OCR pages with at least 100 words and the previous `unverified_ocr` label yielded **zero** row proposals. This does not mean the pages contain no results; the scanned headers and cell recognition are too inconsistent for the current full-grid rule. No new structured polling-row count should be claimed from this mapper yet.
- One preserved Tamil Nadu page had just one confidently read token (`22`) but was labelled `unverified_ocr`. Future OCR batches now mark pages with fewer than 20 words `needs_visual_review`; the strict mapper also rejects sparse previously saved pages. Earlier OCR files remain preserved with their original hashes and need a separately verified reclassification before import.
- The preserved Tamil Nadu source set also includes an out-of-scope Rajya Sabha member list. Future polling OCR batches skip clear Rajya Sabha/Council of States labels while retaining the original source.
- Do not promote OCR proposals to published results without visual review. The ruled-grid pilot below is a first layout-specific adapter, not general Form 20 coverage.

## Ruled-grid pilot on an official Himachal Pradesh Form 20 page

- A bounded test of the new `pilot/cell_ocr_form20.py` adapter used the preserved 2022 Indora (SC) PDF. At 130 dpi, pages 1 and 3 yielded 22 and 23 **unverified** numeric proposals from 25 detected rows each. Candidate sums match printed valid votes and valid/rejected/NOTA sums match printed totals. Unreadable or discrepant rows were omitted; no missing digits were supplied. Page 2 has a visibly similar grid but yielded zero proposals with the default cell reading, so this layout is not complete even within one PDF.
- The first three numeric rows on page 1 were checked against the rendered official page and match. Header OCR read **HANS RAT** where the PDF says **HANS RAJ**. Therefore candidate labels remain `ocr_header` evidence and are not published as candidate names. The proposal JSON is a local review artifact under `exports/indora-form20-page1-cell-proposals.json` with a SHA-256 sidecar; it is not in the live database.
- The adapter currently accepts only a clearly ruled, two-leading-column Form 20 layout with explicit valid/rejected/NOTA/total/tendered headers. Other layouts fail closed. Before scaling, visually review a stratified sample across states and years, measure false positives, and add source-specific layout handling rather than relaxing reconciliation.

## 24 September correction to the ruled-grid pilot

- A higher-scale OCR check of Indora page 2 misread printed polling station **35** as **39** while copying that row's other cells correctly. Both vote arithmetic checks passed, so arithmetic alone did not protect the station identity. The v1 proposals are research artifacts and must not be promoted without visual checking.
- Adapter v2 now requires the two separately printed leading station-number cells to be present and equal. On the same PDF at the default cell scale, v2 retained 21 unverified proposals on page 1 and 21 on page 3, down from 22 and 23; page 2 still yielded none. This deliberately omits rows when OCR cannot confirm identity. No proposed row was added to the published results or accepted candidate tables.
